06Pricing Analysis & Recommendations
Market context. The $251 glamping industry ADR benchmark is weighted toward high-density drive markets (Catskills, Asheville, Hill Country TX). Flint Creek's comparable is a 2-hr drive from Tulsa/OKC — a smaller, lower-density catchment than the benchmark average. A peer like Promised Land, PA (2 hrs from NYC/Philly) runs ~$220 ADR at significantly higher occupancy, reflecting a deeper demand pool. This suggests Flint Creek's pricing may be reaching the ceiling of what a thin market can sustain at current awareness levels — the right move is expanding the addressable audience before pushing rate further.

The Weekday Floor Problem

  • Weekday bookings within 7 days are yielding $155–$188 ADR — anecdotally, rates are dropping to ~$129 midweek close-in, which confirms the data
  • Same-day weekday ADR is $155 vs. $203 for same-day weekends — the weekend rate floor is holding, but weekday has essentially collapsed
  • One possible read: guests find the initial rate too high, return when it drops to a level they can justify, and convert at that point — a pricing sensitivity signal rather than strategic waiting
  • The fix is not eliminating last-minute discounts — it's raising the floor so the discount lands at $169–$179, not $129
  • Weekdays are a different problem than weekends — they need a demand-generation solution (packages, midweek promos) not a rate cut

Where to Apply Pressure

  • Protect weekends aggressively — Fri/Sat guests book 90d+ at $270+ and same-day at $203; the premium audience is already there
  • Mar 2026 at 27.2% occupancy and $245 ADR = $66.45 RevPAR shows spring break demand pulling forward — occupancy nearly doubled vs. Feb's 14%, with rate holding firm
  • Raise the weekday floor to $159–$169 minimum; accept empty midweek nights rather than training guests to expect $129
  • Target premium drive markets: NWA/Fayetteville (large affluent base, only 1.5 hrs) is underutilized vs. OKC/Tulsa
  • Repeat guests are the lowest-cost midweek lever — 619 past guests, 4.7% repeat rate, near-zero cost to reach via email
  • May–Jun 2026 forward bookings show 37–93 day medians — advance planning is building as brand matures; protect that with rate, don't discount it
Key Takeaway
Weekends are working. Same-day weekend ADR holds at $203, advance weekend bookings hit $270+. The problem is weekday midweek, where close-in discounting to ~$129 is eroding blended ADR and training last-minute behavior. The strategy should be: protect and grow weekends, raise the weekday floor, and invest in brand reach rather than rate cuts to solve for occupancy.

08 Group Bookings — The Shoulder Month Lever
The math is compelling. A single group booking of 6 units for 2 midweek nights at rack rate generates ~$2,400–$3,000 in revenue — the equivalent of filling every Tuesday and Wednesday for an entire month at current conversion rates. This is additive revenue that doesn't require discounting, doesn't train last-minute behavior, and directly solves the weekday occupancy gap in shoulder months.

Two Ways Groups Unlock Commons Revenue

  • Active marketing: Bachelorette parties, corporate retreats, family reunions within 3 hrs (Tulsa, OKC, NWA) are a natural fit — they want proximity and shared experience, which Commons delivers at an accessible group rate
  • Stay-extension offers: When a group is already on property for a weekend, a discounted extra night (e.g. "add Sunday night at 25% off") turns a would-be empty night into incremental revenue — no new guest acquisition needed
  • The data already shows clusters of 6–9 units booked simultaneously across Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct — this demand is arriving unprompted; formalizing a group inquiry channel would capture more of it
  • Commons at $160–$193 in a group block is additive revenue, not discounted — the comparison is $160 vs. $0, not $160 vs. $220

Entering Peak Season: Plan, Don't React

  • Mar 2026 spring break shows what demand-driven occupancy looks like — the April dip that follows is normal post-event normalization, not a trend reversal
  • May and June forward bookings show $301 and $253 ADR — guests booking 37–93 days out at premium rates; reactive discounting now would undercut what's already working
  • July's $395 ADR on 10 forward bookings signals strong rate appetite for peak — protect it
  • The shoulder month strategy (group outreach, direct email to past guests, packages) should be built in the next 60 days — not improvised when September occupancy drops
09 Packages & Upsells — A Starting Framework
Packages serve two purposes simultaneously. For guests, they reduce decision friction and increase perceived value. For the property, they anchor a higher booking price, move inventory that might otherwise discount, and create a reason to reach out to past guests with something new. The items below are conversation starters — the right packages for Flint Creek will come from understanding what guests are already asking for on arrival.

🌿 Wellness Escape

Couples · Creekside units
  • Private sauna session (timed, pre-stocked with towels)
  • In-unit welcome kit: candles, face masks, bath salts, herbal tea
  • Late checkout option (11am → 1pm) to extend the wind-down
  • Positioned as an add-on at booking: "Complete the experience"

🏕️ Family Adventure Pack

Families · Commons units
  • River floats (2 per adult, 1 per child) with local outfitter partnership
  • Welcome basket: s'mores kit, colouring books, nature activity cards
  • Early check-in option to maximize the day
  • Bundles the Commons unit at a price point that feels premium, not budget

🥂 Celebration Package

Bachelorettes · Birthdays · Groups
  • Charcuterie board or welcome snack on arrival
  • Branded Ferncrest amenity kit (candle, card, small keepsake)
  • Group rate structure: base rate + flat package fee per booking
  • Natural pipeline into group bookings — one booker, multiple units
Questions to explore with the franchisee
— What are guests most commonly asking for on arrival that isn't currently offered?
— Is there a local float outfitter on the Illinois River open to a referral arrangement?
— What does the sauna capacity and scheduling look like — can it be reserved per session?
— What's the camp store restocking process — could it support a curated welcome kit SKU?
